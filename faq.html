Everything you need to know about Mangrove — how it works, how it's built, and how it keeps your funds safe.

General

Mangrove targets self-directed investors and RIAs. It serves retail crypto investors, aspiring quants, busy professionals, and registered investment advisors seeking safer automated trading without black-box solutions.
Mangrove aims to become "the most trusted platform for digital asset management" by combining AI with professional-grade risk controls and accessibility, allowing everyday investors to automate portfolios with confidence.

Safety is central to the design. Key protections include: AI never controls your funds, the platform maintains no custody of your assets, strategies include built-in risk controls, and the system is designed for SOC 2 compliance aligned with NIST standards.

Strategy Building & AI

Security & Trust

Mangrove is not currently a financial advisor, broker-dealer, or investment company, but is building with regulatory alignment in mind. The platform is actively pursuing SOC 1 and SOC 2 compliance and is designed to operate within emerging regulatory frameworks.
Yes. External wallet or exchange connections are currently required for trade execution. Mangrove never holds your funds — your assets stay in your own accounts at all times.
Trust is built into the product, not just the marketing. Key pillars: compliance infrastructure built from day one, AI safety features that keep automation from ever touching funds autonomously, transparent analytics on every strategy and trade, and a reputation system for creators and strategies on the marketplace.

Monetization

Yes. You can publish your strategies to the Mangrove marketplace and earn a performance fee every time a subscriber profits from your work. The better your strategy performs, the more you earn.
You publish a strategy, other traders subscribe to it, and when they win — you win. Earnings are distributed automatically based on the performance of your strategy across all subscribers.
Yes. You can run strategies entirely privately for your own use. If you choose to publish, you can still protect the underlying logic — subscribers follow the strategy's signals without seeing the code behind it.
Mangrove earns through a small fee (0.85%) on profitable trades and a share (20%) of earnings from monetized strategies. If your trades aren't profitable, Mangrove doesn't collect. The incentives are fully aligned.
